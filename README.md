## 📖 Overview

**Our Little Universe** (Our First Chapter) is an emotional, interactive storytelling journey structured as **9 distinct, cinematic chapters**. Designed like a luxury romantic digital scrapbook and love diary, it celebrates 365 days of togetherness with rich paper textures, starlit celestial paths, interactive origami love notes, blooming flower prayers, keepsake digital letters, and an acoustic music box soundtrack.

---

## ✨ The 9 Interactive Chapters

### 💌 Chapter 1: A Little Surprise (The Welcome)
- **Atmosphere**: Minimal romantic opening screen with blush bokeh, floating rose petals, and warm lighting.
- **Interactive Envelope**: A sealed digital stationery envelope with a pulsing glowing heart wax seal.
- **Action**: Tapping the envelope or *"Open Your Surprise ❤️"* initiates an unfolding paper animation, paper rustle audio, and transitions into Chapter II.

### 📜 Chapter 2: A Letter from My Heart (The Love Letter)
- **Parchment Presentation**: Vintage deckled-edge paper styling with golden filigree corners, dashed inner borders, and soft rose shadows.
- **Authentic Bengali Typography**: Rendered with *Noto Serif Bengali* and *Hind Siliguri* for balanced line heights and clear Bengali Unicode glyphs.
- **Emotional Message**: Preserves the complete original Bengali anniversary message addressed to **Rabeya** (*"প্রিয় রাবেয়া ❤️ — আমাদের প্রথম বছরের গল্প"*).
- **Smooth Pacing**: Natural paragraph fades without forced delays.

### 📷 Chapter 3: Beautiful Memories (Three Precious Photographs)
- **Polaroid-Style Scrapbook**: Real photographs presented as tactile Polaroids with realistic drop shadows, washi tape pins, and subtle rotations (`-3°`, `+2.5°`, `-2°`).
- **Interactive Lightbox**: Hover tilt/parallax effects and tap-to-enlarge lightbox modals for high-resolution photo viewing.
- **Authentic & Respectful**: Preserves her actual photographs without facial alteration or invented dates and locations.

### 💖 Chapter 4: Why You're Special (Origami Love Notes Jar)
- **Keepsake Jar Discovery**: Inspired by folded pastel paper hearts in a vintage glass keepsake jar.
- **Interactive Unfolding**: 6 folded origami heart cards that unfold with gentle audio harp arpeggios when tapped.
- **Meaningful Compliments**: Uncovers sincere appreciations (Radiant Smile, Gentle Heart, Peaceful Sanctuary, Unwavering Patience, Joy in Small Things, Answered Prayer).
- **Progress Meter**: Tracks unlocked love notes in real time.

### ✨ Chapter 5: 365 Days Together (Constellation Timeline)
- **Celestial Starmap**: An interactive celestial starmap timeline celebrating our first complete year together.
- **Interactive Milestones**: Tap each constellation node to journey through:
  - *Day 1 · The Beginning* (Where our story began)
  - *Month 3 · Deepening Conversations* (Finding harmony)
  - *Month 6 · Halfway Around the Sun* (Unshakeable trust)
  - *Month 9 · Through Every Season* (Steadfast companionship)
  - *365 Days · Our 1st Anniversary* (Our complete first chapter)
- **Poetic Accents**: Includes romantic quotes and highlighted glowing starlight nodes.

### 🌸 Chapter 6: A Garden of Wishes (Blooming Prayers)
- **Magical Botanical Garden**: An ornate blooming sanctuary featuring 6 flowers:
  - *Crimson Rose (গোলাপ)*: Eternal devotion & sincere affection
  - *White Jasmine (বেলি)*: Purity, peace & serenity
  - *Golden Peony (পিয়নিয়া)*: Prosperity & realization of dreams
  - *Water Lotus (পদ্ম)*: Patience & resilient harmony
  - *Sweet Lavender (ল্যাভেন্ডার)*: Grace & everlasting comfort
  - *Pink Orchid (অর্কিড)*: Graceful beauty & answered prayers
- **Interactive Blooms**: Tapping each blossom plays a crystal glockenspiel chime, blooming the flower with a glowing aura and revealing sincere Bengali prayers and English meanings.

### 📬 Chapter 7: Open When You Need Love (Keepsake Envelopes)
- **5 Digital Envelopes**:
  1. *Open When You're Sad* ("যখন তোমার মন খারাপ...")
  2. *Open When You Miss Me* ("যখন আমাকে খুব মিস করবে...")
  3. *Open When You Need a Smile* ("যখন তোমার এক চিলতে হাসি দরকার...")
  4. *Open When You Need Encouragement* ("যখন তোমার সাহস ও প্রেরণা প্রয়োজন...")
  5. *Open When You Want to Feel Loved* ("যখন তুমি ভালোবাসার পরশ পেতে চাও...")
- **Warm Solace**: Detailed comforting letters in natural Bengali and English, available to open, close, and reread whenever comfort is needed.

### 🎉 Chapter 8: Our First Anniversary (Grand Celebration)
- **Main Centerpiece**: Bold **"1 YEAR"** display in metallic champagne gold *Playfair Display* typography with luminous gleam.
- **Supporting Text**: *"365 days of love, memories & togetherness ❤️"*.
- **Celebration Effects**:

### 🌌 Chapter 9: Forever Starts Here (The Cinematic Finale)

- **Secret Starlight Easter Egg**: A hidden button revealing a secret starlight whisper message for Rabeya.
- **Seamless Replay**: An intuitive *"Replay Our Story ↺"* button that resets the storytelling journey back to the beginning.

## 🎵 Music & Audio Experience

- **Royalty-Free Music Box Synthesizer**: Built using the native Web Audio API (`RomanticAudioEngine`), synthesizing an acoustic music box arpeggio in D Major cadence without requiring external downloads or third-party audio hosting.
- **Persistent Playback**: Background audio plays continuously across all 9 scene transitions without interruptions.
- **Global Audio & Chapter Navigation**:
  - Play / Pause toggle.
  - Continuous volume slider (0% to 100%) and one-click mute.
  - Animated dancing soundwave bars reflecting real-time playback state.
  - Quick `<` Previous and `>` Next chapter navigation buttons.
  - Story progress bar indicating current chapter out of 9.
  - 9-Chapter jump drawer with custom icons and descriptions.
- **Autoplay Compliance**: Follows modern browser autoplay restrictions by triggering playback seamlessly upon the user's first natural interaction on the page.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) (v20+ or v22 recommended)
- `npm` (included with Node.js)

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/mijum/Birthday-Present.git
   cd Birthday-Present
   ```

2. **Install dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the local development server**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

4. **Build for production**:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```

5. **Preview the production build**:
   ```bash
   npm run preview
   ```

## 🌐 Deployment Options

### Deploy to Render
1. Create a new **Static Site** on [Render](https://render.com).
2. Connect your GitHub repository.
3. Configure the build parameters:
   - **Build Command**: `npm run build`
   - **Publish Directory**: `dist`
4. Click **Create Static Site**.

### Deploy to Vercel
1. Import the repository into [Vercel](https://vercel.com).
2. Framework Preset will automatically detect **Vite**.
3. Click **Deploy**.

### Deploy to Netlify
1. Connect the repository on [Netlify](https://netlify.com).
2. Build command: `npm run build`
3. Publish directory: `dist`
4. Click **Deploy Site**.
